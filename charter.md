# Agile Team Charter — Book Swap SA

**Module:** PRP372S — Project Presentation 3  
**Institution:** Cape Peninsula University of Technology  
**Project:** Second-Hand Textbook Swap

## 1. Team Purpose

The purpose of Book Swap SA is to provide CPUT students with a convenient platform for buying, selling and exchanging second-hand textbooks. The project aims to make academic resources more affordable and encourage the reuse of learning materials.

## 2. Team Roles and Responsibilities

The team consists of five members:

- **Litha Owethu Mazibuko:** Project Facilitator / Scrum Master.
- **Samkelo Mahlangu:** Product Owner / Content Lead.
- **Coben Maistry:** Frontend Developer.
- **Angelia Van der Westhuizen:** UI/UX and Quality Assurance Lead.
- **Ongeziwe Raqa:** Deployment and Documentation Lead.

These roles are based on the team's existing Agile Team Charter.

## 3. Team Values and Agile Principles

The team values respect, effective communication, collaboration, transparency and constructive feedback. The team aims to adapt to changes, deliver a working product and prioritise the needs of CPUT students.

## 4. Communication Plan

The team's documented communication methods include WhatsApp for daily updates, virtual progress check-ins and GitHub for collaboration and version control. Weekly meetings are used to review progress and coordinate activities.

## 5. Project Goals

The project aims to develop a textbook exchange platform with essential features such as textbook searching, filtering and a responsive website. Further improvements identified in the charter include a wishlist, user guide and administrator dashboard.

## 6. Evidence

The original Agile Team Charter documents the team's purpose, vision, roles, responsibilities, values, communication plan and development goals.

The original charter should be retained as supporting evidence for this portfolio section.

## 7. Reflection

The Agile Team Charter establishes a shared understanding of the project's purpose and the responsibilities of each team member. It also highlights the importance of communication and collaboration throughout development.

Having defined roles helps the team coordinate its work, while the project goals provide direction for planning and prioritising features. The charter can be used as a reference when reviewing progress and ensuring that development remains focused on the needs of CPUT students.
